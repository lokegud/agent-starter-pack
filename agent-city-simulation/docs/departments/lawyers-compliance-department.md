# Lawyers & Compliance Department
*"Where successful products become legally compliant businesses."*

## Core Concept
Activates when product gains traction (1,000+ users or $10k+ revenue) → Ensures legal compliance

## Activation Triggers
- 1,000+ users (privacy laws apply)
- $10,000+ revenue (financial regulations)
- Collecting user data (GDPR, CCPA)
- Processing payments (PCI-DSS)
- High-risk domain (healthcare, finance)

## Team Composition (4 Roles)

**1. Privacy Lawyer** (0.5 per project)
- GDPR, CCPA compliance, privacy policies
- Example: Rebecca Thompson (risk_tolerance: 0.15, analytical: 0.92)
- Ensures user data handled legally

**2. Terms & Conditions Specialist** (0.5 per project)
- User agreements, liability protection
- Example: David Kim (analytical: 0.88, assertiveness: 0.81)
- Protects company from legal liability

**3. IP Lawyer** (0.25 per project)
- Trademark, copyright, patent protection
- Example: Maria Garcia (patience: 0.89, analytical: 0.85)
- Protects intellectual property

**4. Compliance Auditor** (0.25 per project)
- Ongoing compliance monitoring
- Example: James Chen (risk_tolerance: 0.20, assertiveness: 0.84)
- Ensures continued compliance

## Compliance Checklist

**Privacy (GDPR/CCPA)**:
✅ Privacy policy published  
✅ Cookie consent mechanism  
✅ Data retention policy  
✅ User data deletion on request  
✅ Data breach notification plan  

**Terms of Service**:
✅ Liability limitations  
✅ User conduct rules  
✅ Dispute resolution process  
✅ Refund policy (if applicable)  

**International Compliance**:
✅ GDPR (EU users)  
✅ CCPA (California users)  
✅ Age verification (COPPA for <13)  

## Workflow (1-2 weeks)

**Week 1: Review**
- Audit current product practices
- Identify compliance gaps
- Draft required legal documents

**Week 2: Implementation**
- Add cookie consent banners
- Update privacy policy
- Add ToS acceptance flow
- Document data handling practices

## Personality Impact
