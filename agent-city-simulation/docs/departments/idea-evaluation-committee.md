# Idea Evaluation Committee - The Expert Panel
*"Where scout findings become validated, researched opportunities with actionable blueprints."*

## 🎯 Core Concept

When Huginn and Muninn return from their 10-hour scout cycle, their raw findings enter a **rigorous multi-stage evaluation process**:

1. **Research Phase**: Deep investigation, knowledge base building
2. **Expert Panel**: 9 specialists + 1 builder rank and brainstorm
3. **Tiered Processing**: High-potential → Experts, Lower-tier → Trainees
4. **Output**: Fully researched, ranked, brainstormed ideas ready for building/trading

**Think of it as:** A venture capital investment committee that doesn't just evaluate, but actively designs the solution.

## 📊 The Three-Stage Pipeline

```
STAGE 1: RESEARCH & KNOWLEDGE BASE
Scout Findings → Research Agents → Comprehensive Dossier
    ↓
STAGE 2: EXPERT PANEL RANKING
Dossier → 9 Experts + 1 Builder → Money Potential Score
    ↓
STAGE 3: BRAINSTORMING & BLUEPRINT
High-Tier (>0.70) → Expert Panel Brainstorm
Low-Tier (0.50-0.70) → Training Panel Brainstorm
Below Threshold (<0.50) → Archive
    ↓
OUTPUT: Actionable Ideas with Implementation Plans
```
